STEP 4 · ASSETS (optional)

Things you want on the slides exactly as they are:
logos, icons, app screenshots, stickers, arrows, your signature, a product shot.

Best formats:
- PNG with a transparent background, at least 1000 px wide
- JPG or PNG on a plain white background: the white gets cut out for you
- SVG works too: Claude converts it to a PNG first

Extras you can ask for: a white sticker outline, or a big red X to cross something out.

Name files so they're easy to ask for: logo.svg, app-icon.png, arrow-hand-drawn.svg
Claude places them as they are. It won't redraw, recolor or invent them.

Only assets you have the right to use.
