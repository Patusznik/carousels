# Cheat sheet: what to say

Talk normally, in any language. Claude turns it into edits and shows you the result.

## Start

| say | what happens |
|---|---|
| `/carousels` | the brief: what it's about, who it's for, your hook, then your carousel folder opens |
| "ready" | Claude reads your folders and proposes a slide plan |
| "here's my text: …" | your script becomes slides, layouts picked for you |
| "I'll dictate the slides" | you go slide by slide, Claude builds and shows each one |
| "help me with the hook" | three hook options in different styles |
| "continue *One notebook*" | picks up an existing carousel |

## Slides

| say | what you get |
|---|---|
| "cover on the coffee photo, title behind my head" | `cover` |
| "cover with me as a sticker" | `cover-sticker` |
| "full photo, text at the bottom" | `photo` |
| "photo on top, text below" | `split` |
| "just text" / "with a small sticker of me" | `text` |
| "a list: …, …, …" | `list` |
| "a quote on dark" | `quote` |
| "end with save / share / comment" | `cta` |

## Mockups (macOS / iPhone style)

| say | what you get |
|---|---|
| "a chat bubble on the right: …, and a reply on the left: …" | two `bubble`s |
| "a pop-up asking …, buttons Later and Show me" | `dialog` |
| "a notification from Messages: …" | `notif` |
| "a terminal window with …" | `window` with terminal lines |
| "a Force Quit window listing …" | `window` with rows and a button |
| "put that window behind me" | `layer=behind`: it shows around you, not over you |
| "put the coffee from assets bottom left" | your file as an `image`, white background cut out |
| "give it a sticker outline" / "cross out the laptop" | white sticker edge / big red X |
| "a cursor clicking the button" | `cursor` |

## Text

| say | what you get |
|---|---|
| "break the title after *notebook*" | new line in the title |
| "make *week* italic" / "in the accent color" | accent italic |
| "bold *free*" | **free** in the body |
| "bigger / smaller title" | title size |
| "small label on top of every slide: SERIES NAME" | `kicker` on all slides |
| "no slide numbers" | counter off |

## Photos and framing

| say | what you get |
|---|---|
| "use the coffee photo on slide 3" | different photo (names are on the photo preview) |
| "zoom in" / "zoom out" | crop zoom |
| "move the crop up / down / left / right" | focus point |
| "head deeper into the title" / "my head covers the title" | cover overlap |
| "make me a sticker on slide 5" | you, cut out, white outline |

## Order

| say | what happens |
|---|---|
| "add a slide after 4: …" | new slide 5, the rest shift |
| "swap 2 and 3" / "move 6 to the start" | new order |
| "delete 7" / "duplicate 3" | remove / copy |
| "show me everything" | all slides side by side |
| "show slide 4 big" | one slide full size |

## Look

| say | what happens |
|---|---|
| "make it look like my reference carousel" | Claude reads it and builds a theme in your colors, shows old vs new |
| "warmer / cooler / more faded photos" | photo grade |
| "less grain" / "no grain" | grain |
| "use Avenir for the text" | any font on your computer, by name |
| "update my brand kit" | change colors, fonts, handle for every future carousel |

Finished files land in your carousel's `final/` folder: `01.png`, `02.png`… (1080×1350, upload in that order),
`carousel.pdf` and `sheet.jpg`.
