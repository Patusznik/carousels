STEP 3 · PHOTOS (the only must-have)

Your photos for this carousel. Straight from your phone is fine (HEIC, JPG, PNG). Short videos work too:
Claude pulls the sharpest frames.

Quick tips:
- shoot vertical, 1x lens, light from the front or side
- cover photo: waist up with lots of space above your head (the title goes behind it)
- sticker photo: full body or from the knees, arms away from your body, plain background
- for slides with chat bubbles or windows: face in the top half of the frame
- 15 to 30 photos in one go, same light. You'll pick from a preview.

Only photos you have the right to use. Ask before using photos of other people.
