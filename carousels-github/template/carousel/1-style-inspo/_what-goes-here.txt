STEP 1 · STYLE INSPO (optional)

Drop screenshots of the look you like here.
Pinterest is perfect for this: save pins, then screenshot them.

Good things to collect:
- color palettes and moods
- fonts and title styles you like
- macOS / iPhone UI mockups (chat bubbles, pop-ups, windows, notifications)
- photo treatments (warm, faded film, black and white, grain, cut-outs)

5 to 15 images is plenty. Claude reads the mood from them, it doesn't copy any single one.
Empty is fine: Claude uses your brand kit instead.
